## Concepts

Returns the flows registered in the WABA, with name, categories and publication status.

The list comes from the platform snapshot. When it is empty, the listing syncs with Meta before responding.

With `sync=true`, the listing syncs every flow with Meta before responding, like [Sync flows](/en/flows/sync-flows). It is slower, but brings the current state.

<Warning>
  This route has no pagination: `limit` is ignored, and the response brings every flow at once.
</Warning>

<Note>
  The `wabaId` is obtained through the [List WABAs](/en/templates/list-businesses) endpoint.
</Note>
