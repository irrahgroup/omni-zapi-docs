## Concepts

Returns the flows registered in the WABA, with name, categories and publication status.

The list comes complete, in a single response: there is no pagination. Without parameters, it comes from the platform's records. With `sync=true`, the platform syncs the flows with Meta before listing, which is slower but brings the updated status.

<Note>
  The `wabaId` is obtained through the [List WABAs](/en/templates/list-businesses) endpoint.
</Note>
