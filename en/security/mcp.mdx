**Administration →** [Connected apps](https://app.omni.z-api.io/app/connected-apps) holds the outside programs that someone in the workspace authorized to use the account, without handing over a password or the Secret Key. They connect in two ways:

- **Apps, through OAuth:** integrations registered by the platform, such as partner integrations. They use the {projectName} API with the permissions you authorized, which can include sending messages. Connecting an app through OAuth is a separate topic and is not covered on this page.
- **AIs, through MCP:** AI assistants that use the workspace through the MCP server. They are a case within connected apps.

This page covers the second way.

## Connect an AI

{projectName} has an MCP (Model Context Protocol) server. With it, an AI assistant such as Claude, ChatGPT, Cursor, VS Code or n8n can use your workspace. In this version, the AI looks up channels, templates, flows, webhooks and billing. The full list is below.

The AI never gets your Secret Key. You sign in with your account, choose the workspace and authorize the access. You can revoke that access at any time.

## Server address

```text
https://mcp.omni.z-api.io/mcp
```

Use this address in any AI client that supports a remote MCP server. Authentication goes through your account sign-in (OAuth), with no key to copy.

## How the connection works

1. You add the address to the AI client.
2. The client opens the {projectName} sign-in page. Sign in with your account.
3. Choose the workspace the AI will read.
4. Check what the AI will be able to do.
5. Click to authorize.

The access shows up in **Administration →** [Connected apps](https://app.omni.z-api.io/app/connected-apps), in the **Apps connected to the workspace** section, along with who authorized it. To cut the access, click **Revoke**.

<Note>
  The AI acts with the permissions of the person who authorized it and disconnects when that person leaves the workspace.
</Note>

  </Tab>
  <Tab title="VS Code">
    Add the server to VS Code's `mcp.json` file:

    ```json mcp.json

      "servers": {
        "omni-zapi": {

      }
    ```

    When the server starts, VS Code opens the {projectName} sign-in.

## What the AI can do

In this version, the AI can use the tools below. They all look up data; the validation tools check a template or a flow and return the errors, without creating, sending or syncing anything.

| Permission | Tools |
|---|---|
| **See channels** | `account_whoami` (account details), `channel_types_list`, `channels_list`, `channels_get`, `webhooks_list` |
| **See templates** | `templates_list`, `templates_get`, `templates_validate` |
| **See flows** | `flows_list`, `flows_get`, `flows_validate` |
| **See billing and plans** | `plans_list`, `billing_summary` |

New operations will be added to MCP. What the AI does always depends on the workspace policy's **Maximum permissions**: it never gets a permission that isn't checked there. Without **See billing and plans**, for example, it doesn't look up billing or plans.

Every AI call is recorded in the [Audit log](/en/security/audit), with the **Connected AI** actor.

## Workspace policy

In **Administration →** [Connected apps](https://app.omni.z-api.io/app/connected-apps), click **See the policy**. Only people who manage the workspace apps can change the policy.

| Option | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Allow apps in this workspace** | Turns all connected apps on or off. Turning off apps also turns off the AIs. |
| **Connected AIs** | Turns AI access through MCP on or off. |
| **Accept unverified AIs** | Lets in AIs without the verified badge, such as Cursor and n8n. |
| **Allowed apps** | Any app in the catalog or only the chosen apps. With only the chosen apps, the list decides. |
| **Maximum permissions** | The most an app gets: **See channels**, **See templates**, **See flows** and **See billing and plans**. |
| Message limit, access validity and two-step verification | Message limit per day per app, how many days the access lasts and whether whoever authorizes needs [two-step verification](/en/security/two-factor) turned on. |
| **Serve AIs even outside the IP list** | Only shows up when the workspace has an [IP list](/en/security/ip-restriction). |

## Security

- **Unverified AIs:** they only get in with **Accept unverified AIs** turned on or with the app allowed in **Allowed apps**. Claude, ChatGPT and VS Code are verified.
- **IP list:** AI requests come from the AI provider's servers, not from your network. If the workspace has [IP restriction](/en/security/ip-restriction), access by AI is refused unless **Serve AIs even outside the IP list** is on.
- **Cut every AI at once:** turn off **Connected AIs**. No access is revoked: the other apps stay connected through the public API.
- **Cut one AI:** in **Apps connected to the workspace**, click **Revoke** on the app. It only comes back if someone authorizes it again.
