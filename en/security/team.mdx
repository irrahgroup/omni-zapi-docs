In {projectName}, each person on the team has a **role** in the workspace. The role defines what they see and what they can change in the dashboard. The team is in **Administration → Team**.

## Roles

| Role | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Owner** | Owns the workspace. Can do everything, including deleting the workspace and transferring ownership. |
| **Administrator** | Can do everything except deleting the workspace and transferring ownership. |
| **Access admin** | Takes care of people and security: invites and removes people, gives the Viewer role, sees the Audit log and Security, and changes the IP restriction. Does not see channels or billing. |
| **Developer** | Takes care of integrations: sees channels, the channel token, templates and webhooks, changes webhooks, connected apps and AIs, and sees Security. Does not change channels. |
| **Channel operator** | Runs channels and templates: sees and changes channels and templates, and sees the channel token, webhooks and connected apps. |
| **Finance** | Sees and changes billing. |
| **Viewer** | Only looks: channels, templates, webhooks, connected apps, team, billing, Audit log and Security. Changes nothing and does not see the channel token. |

Each workspace has a single Owner. Nobody gives this role: it only moves to another person through the [ownership transfer](#transfer-ownership).

### Manager and Member

People who were already on the team before roles appear as **Manager** or **Member**:

- **Manager:** sees and changes channels, templates and webhooks, sees the channel token and connected apps, invites and removes Members, and sees billing and the Audit log.
- **Member:** sees channels, the channel token, templates, webhooks and connected apps. Changes nothing.

Manager and Member cannot be given to other people. When someone [edits the access](#edit-access) of a Manager or a Member, the person gets the chosen role.

## Invite people

The **Owner**, the **Administrator**, the **Access admin** and the **Manager** can invite.

1. In the dashboard, open **Administration → Team** and click **Invite people**.
2. Enter the emails. Separate them with commas to invite several people at once.
3. Under **In this workspace**, choose the role. None is selected by default. When you choose one, the dashboard shows what the person will be able to see and change.
4. Click **Continue**, review the list and the role, and click **Send invitations**.

You only give roles whose permissions you also have. The others appear dimmed:

- The Owner and the Administrator give any role except Owner.
- The Access admin gives only Viewer.
- The Manager invites only as Member.

All emails in the same batch get the same role. Each person receives their own link by email. The invitation:

- **Expires in 7 days.** After that, send a new one.
- Carries the chosen role: the person joins the workspace with it.
- Is not sent to people who are already in the workspace or already have a pending invitation.
- Stops being valid if, before it is accepted, the person who sent it leaves the team or loses the right to give that role.

## Edit access

The **Owner**, the **Administrator** and the **Access admin** edit other people's access. In **Administration → Team**, open the actions menu (⋮) on the person's row, click **Edit access**, choose the role and click **Save**.

You only edit people whose roles you reach:

- The Owner and the Administrator edit anyone except the Owner.
- The Access admin edits Developers, Channel operators, Finance, Viewers, Managers and Members. They don't edit an Administrator or another Access admin.

If the person has a role you can't give, that role appears selected and doesn't change.

The change takes effect as soon as you save and is recorded in the [Audit log](/en/security/audit). The Owner and the person receive an email. With the change:

- Pending invitations the person sent that the new role would not allow are canceled.
- If the new role doesn't allow granting write access to apps, the connected apps the person authorized with write access lose it.

## Remove a person

In the actions menu (⋮) on the person's row, click **Remove** and confirm. The Owner, the Administrator, the Access admin and the Manager can remove people, but only those they reach, with the same rules as [Edit access](#edit-access). The Manager removes Members only.

The person loses access to the workspace. Pending invitations they sent are canceled, and the connected apps they authorized in this workspace are disconnected.

<Note>
  Nobody can remove themselves or change their own access. The Owner's row has no actions menu: nobody changes their role or removes them.
</Note>

## Transfer ownership

Only the **Owner** transfers ownership, to any other active person on the team:

1. In **Administration → Team**, open the actions menu (⋮) on the person's row and click **Transfer ownership**.
2. Review what changes and type the workspace name to confirm.

With the transfer:

- The chosen person becomes the Owner of the workspace.
- You get the Administrator role.
- The connected apps you authorized in this workspace with write or send permission are disconnected.
- You both receive an email.

## Track invitations

On the **Team** screen, filter the list by status (**All**, **Active**, **Invited** and **Expired**) or by **Role**. Each invitation shows the role it was sent with and how many days are left before it expires.

In the actions menu (⋮) of each invitation:

- **Resend invite:** sends a new link, valid for another 7 days. The previous link stops working. It only appears for people who could send that invitation.
- **Cancel invite:** on a pending invitation, the link that was sent stops working.
- **Delete invite:** on an expired invitation, removes it from the list.

## Accept an invitation

The invited person opens the link in the email or, in the dashboard, the **profile menu → Invitations**, and clicks **Accept** or **Decline**. The invitation shows the role the person will join with. After accepting, the workspace appears in the workspace selector.

<Info>
  The person joins the workspace with the role in the invitation. If they need a different role later, someone who can edit access changes it in [Edit access](#edit-access).
</Info>
