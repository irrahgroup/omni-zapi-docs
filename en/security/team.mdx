In {projectName}, each person on the team has a **role** in the workspace. The role defines what they see and what they can change in the dashboard. The team is in **Administration → Team**.

## Roles

### Owner

Workspace owner. Has access to **everything**, including what no other role can do:

- Manage billing: subscribe to channels, pay invoices and change the payment method.
- Access the [Security](/en/security/introduction) menu: API credentials, IP restriction and SDK configuration.
- Promote a Member to Manager or demote a Manager to Member.
- Invite people directly as Manager.
- Transfer ownership of the workspace to another person on the team.

Each workspace has a single Owner. The Owner cannot be removed from the workspace, and their role only changes through an [ownership transfer](#transfer-ownership).

### Manager

Runs the day-to-day operation, without access to billing and security:

- Creates, edits and deletes channels and templates.
- Invites people as Member, resends and cancels invitations.
- Removes **only Members**. Cannot remove another Manager or the Owner.
- Sees billing, without changing it, and checks the [Audit log](/en/security/audit).

### Member

Follows the operation, without changing anything:

- Sees channels and templates.
- Does not access Team, billing, Audit log or Security.

### Permissions summary

| Action | Owner | Manager | Member |
|---|:---:|:---:|:---:|
| See channels and templates | ✅ | ✅ | ✅ |
| Create, edit and delete channels and templates | ✅ | ✅ | ❌ |
| See the team and invitations | ✅ | ✅ | ❌ |
| Invite as Member, resend and cancel invitations | ✅ | ✅ | ❌ |
| Invite as Manager | ✅ | ❌ | ❌ |
| Remove members | ✅ | Members only | ❌ |
| Promote and demote roles | ✅ | ❌ | ❌ |
| Transfer ownership of the workspace | ✅ | ❌ | ❌ |
| See billing | ✅ | ✅ | ❌ |
| Manage billing | ✅ | ❌ | ❌ |
| See the Audit log | ✅ | ✅ | ❌ |
| Access Security | ✅ | ❌ | ❌ |

<Note>
  Nobody can remove themselves or change their own role. The exception is the Owner, who becomes a Manager when transferring ownership.
</Note>

## Change a role

Only the **Owner** changes roles. In **Administration → Team**, open the actions menu (⋮) on the person's row:

- **Promote to manager:** turns a Member into a Manager.
- **Relegate to member:** turns a Manager into a Member.

Before the change, the dashboard asks for confirmation and shows what the person will be able to do with the new role. The change takes effect as soon as you confirm and is recorded in the [Audit log](/en/security/audit). To take the person out of the workspace, use **Remove**, in the same menu.

<Note>
  The Owner's row has no actions menu: nobody changes their role or removes them.
</Note>

## Transfer ownership

The Owner can hand the workspace over to a Manager or a Member of the team:

1. In **Administration → Team**, open the actions menu (⋮) on the person's row and click **Transfer ownership**.
2. Review what changes and type the workspace name to confirm.

With the transfer:

- The chosen person becomes the Owner of the workspace, with access to billing and the team.
- You become a Manager.
- The connected apps you authorized in this workspace with write or send permission are disconnected.
- You both receive an email.

## Invite people

1. In the dashboard, open **Administration → Team** and click **Invite people**.
2. Enter the emails. Separate them with commas to invite several people at once.
3. Choose the **Role**: **Member**, selected by default, or **Manager**. The Manager option only appears for the Owner.
4. Click **Continue**, review the list and the role, and click **Send invitations**.

All emails in the same batch get the same role. Each person receives their own link by email. The invitation:

- **Expires in 7 days.** After that, send a new one.
- Carries the chosen role: the person joins the workspace with it.
- Is not sent to people who are already in the workspace or already have a pending invitation.

## Track invitations

On the **Team** screen, filter the list by status (**All**, **Active**, **Invited** and **Expired**) or by role. Each invitation shows the role it was sent with and how many days are left before it expires.

In the actions menu (⋮) of each invitation:

- **Resend invite:** sends a new link. The previous link stops working.
- **Cancel invite:** on a pending invitation, the link that was sent stops working.
- **Delete invite:** on an expired invitation, removes it from the list.

## Accept an invitation

The invited person opens the link in the email or, in the dashboard, the **profile menu → Invitations**, and clicks **Accept** or **Decline**. The invitation shows the role the person will join with. After accepting, the workspace appears in the workspace selector.

<Info>
  The person joins the workspace with the role in the invitation. If they need a different role later, the Owner changes it in [Change a role](#change-a-role).
</Info>
