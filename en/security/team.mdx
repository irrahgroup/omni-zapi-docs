In {projectName}, each person on the team has a **role** in the workspace. The role defines what they see and what they can change in the dashboard. The team is in **Administration → Team**.

## Roles

### Owner

Workspace owner. Has access to **everything**, including what no other role can do:

- Manage billing: subscribe to channels, pay invoices and change the payment method.
- Access the [Security](/en/security/introduction) menu: API credentials, IP restriction and SDK configuration.
- Promote a Member to Manager or demote a Manager to Member.

The Owner cannot be removed from the workspace or have their role changed.

### Manager

Runs the day-to-day operation, without access to billing and security:

- Creates, edits and deletes channels and templates.
- Invites people, resends and revokes invitations.
- Removes **only Members**. Cannot remove another Manager or the Owner.
- Sees billing, without changing it, and checks the [Audit log](/en/security/audit).

### Member

Follows the operation, without changing anything:

- Sees channels and templates.
- Does not access Team, billing, Audit log or Security.

### Permissions summary

| Action | Owner | Manager | Member |
|---|:---:|:---:|:---:|
| See channels and templates | ✅ | ✅ | ✅ |
| Create, edit and delete channels and templates | ✅ | ✅ | ❌ |
| See the team and invitations | ✅ | ✅ | ❌ |
| Invite, resend and revoke invitations | ✅ | ✅ | ❌ |
| Remove members | ✅ | Members only | ❌ |
| Promote and demote roles | ✅ | ❌ | ❌ |
| See billing | ✅ | ✅ | ❌ |
| Manage billing | ✅ | ❌ | ❌ |
| See the Audit log | ✅ | ✅ | ❌ |
| Access Security | ✅ | ❌ | ❌ |

<Note>
  Nobody can remove themselves or change their own role.
</Note>

## Change a role

Only the **Owner** changes roles. In **Administration → Team**, in the **Active** section, each person has the action buttons on their own row:

- **Promote to manager:** turns a Member into a Manager.

<Frame>
  <img src="/images/security-team-member.png" alt="Member with the Promote to manager button" />
</Frame>

- **Relegate to member:** turns a Manager into a Member.

<Frame>
  <img src="/images/security-team-manager.png" alt="Manager with the Relegate to member button" />
</Frame>

The change takes effect immediately, with no confirmation step, and is recorded in the [Audit log](/en/security/audit). To take the person out of the workspace, use **Remove**.

<Note>
  The Owner has no action buttons: their role cannot be changed or removed.
</Note>

## Invite people

1. In the dashboard, open **Administration → Team** and click **Invite people**.
2. Enter the emails. Separate them with commas to invite several people at once.
3. Click **Continue**, review the list and click **Send invitations**.

Each person receives their own link by email. The invitation:

- **Expires in 7 days.** After that, send a new one.
- Joins the workspace with the **Member** role. The Owner can promote them to Manager later.
- Is not sent to people who are already in the workspace or already have a pending invitation.

## Track invitations

On the **Team** screen, invitations are grouped into:

- **Active:** people with access.
- **Pending invitations:** waiting for acceptance. You can **Resend** or **Revoke**.
- **Closed:** expired or revoked invitations, kept as history.

## Accept an invitation

The invited person opens the link in the email or, in the dashboard, the **profile menu → Invitations**, and clicks **Accept** or **Decline**. After accepting, the workspace appears in the workspace selector.

<Info>
  Everyone who accepts an invitation joins the workspace as a **Member**, always. You cannot choose another role in the invitation. If they need more access, the Owner promotes them to Manager afterwards, in [Change a role](#change-a-role).
</Info>
