The Audit log records the actions performed in the workspace: who did it, what, when, from which IP and whether it worked. Use it to answer questions such as "who generated a new Secret Key?" or "who deleted this channel?".

The screen is in **Administration → Audit log** and is available to the **Owner** and the **Manager** (see [Team and roles](/en/security/team)).

## What is recorded

- **Who did it (actor):**
  - **User:** a team member, through the dashboard.
  - **Integration:** an API call with your credentials.
  - **System:** an automatic action of the platform.
- **Action, resource, date and time, and source IP.**
- **Outcome:** `SUCCESS`, `FAILURE` or `DENIED` (when permission was missing), with the HTTP status code of the response.

Records are **immutable**: they cannot be changed or deleted.

## Choose the scope

In **Show events from**:

- **The whole workspace:** what any person or integration did in this workspace.
- **Only my actions:** only what you did, in any workspace you belong to.

## Filter

Filter by **Action**, **Actor type**, **Outcome**, **Actor login**, **Resource type** and period (**From** and **To**). Click **Apply** to filter and **Clear** to go back to the full list.

Click an event to open the **Event detail**.

## Export

Click **Export CSV** to download the events shown on the current page of the list, with the filters applied. To export more events, go through the pages and export each one.
