## Overview

New-API contact routes, under `/v1/channels/{channelId}/contacts`. Available **on the WhatsApp Web channel** (`Z_API_WHATSAPP`) **only**: list, get, photo, check numbers, and capabilities.

<Note>
  On a Meta channel (`META_WHATSAPP`), these routes respond `422` — the official API gives no access to the number's contacts. The [capabilities](/en/contacts/capabilities) route responds `200` with an empty operations list. Telegram, Instagram and Messenger also respond `422` on these routes: contacts belong to a WhatsApp number.
</Note>

<Note>
  Writes (add, remove, and report a contact) come in a later phase and respond `422` today.
</Note>

Authentication is the [Secret Key](/en/authentication). Only the `/v1/channels/{channelId}/...` path is served; the `/instances` and `/devices` prefixes respond `404`.

## Common errors

Every contact route may respond, on the **Omni API**: `401` when the Secret Key is refused ([Authentication](/en/authentication)); `403` `ip_not_allowed` when the IP is off the list ([IP restriction](/en/security/ip-restriction)); and `404` `{"error": "instance not found"}` when the channel does not exist.

On the data routes (list, get, photo and check), the checks run in this order: missing channel (`404`), instance not provisioned (`422` `{"error": "instance is not provisioned"}`), subscription inactive (`422` `{"error": "instance subscription is not active"}`), workspace blocked (`422` `{"error": "You are blocked, contact support."}`) and, last, operation not supported on the channel (`422` `operation not supported for this instance`). The capabilities route does not run these `422` checks.

<Note>
  The blocked status is cached for up to 4 hours: blocking or unblocking the workspace may take that long to apply.
</Note>

## Channel errors

On the list, get, photo and check routes, the API relays the request to the channel. Beyond the errors above, they may respond:

- **Channel refusal (`4xx`)**: the same status the channel gave, with `{"error": "provider_error", "message": "<channel text>"}`. The `message` may be missing.
- **`429`**: the same body, with the `Retry-After` header when the channel sends the wait in seconds. Wait that long before trying again.
- **`504`** `{"error": "integration_timeout", "message": "The operation took too long to complete. Try again in a few moments."}`: the channel ran out of time to answer. The route only reads data, so you can repeat the call.
- **`502`** `{"error": "integration_unavailable", ...}`: the connection to the channel failed, the channel answered `401`, `403`, `405` or `5xx`, or it gave an answer outside the expected format.
- **`502`** `{"error": "provider_response_too_large", "message": "..."}`: the channel answer went over the accepted size. On the list route, the `message` asks for a smaller `pageSize` (`The channel answer is too large. Use a smaller pageSize.`); on the check route, for fewer contacts per request (`Send fewer contacts per request.`).
