## Overview

Batch-check whether each number has WhatsApp.

<Note>
  From 1 to 50,000 numbers per call (outside that, `400` `field` `contacts`), with a body of up to 10 MiB (above it, `413` `payload_too_large`). If the channel response is too large, it responds `502` `provider_response_too_large`.
</Note>
