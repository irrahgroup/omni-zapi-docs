## Overview

A contact's data.

<Note>
  An id out of format responds `400` `invalid_request`. A number the channel does not recognize responds with the message `The channel did not recognize this contact: ...` — the text `Phone not exists` only appears when Z-API itself returns 4xx.
</Note>

<Note>
  The detail carries `photoUrl` (valid for ~48h) and does **not** carry `lid`. `phone` is `null` when the contact only has a LID, and then `id` is the LID. Null fields appear in the JSON.
</Note>
