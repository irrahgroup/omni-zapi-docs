## Overview

Method for sending messages with a card carousel. Each card has an image, text, and action buttons. Two button types are accepted:

- **URL**: opens a link
- **REPLY**: quick reply

## Carousel rules

- The carousel has 2 to 10 cards.
- `message` is required and has up to 1,024 characters.
- Each card requires `image`, `text`, and at least one button in `buttons`.
- `image` is a URL or a JPEG or PNG image in base64 (data URI) of up to 5 MB.
- `text` has up to 160 characters and at most 2 line breaks.
- `label` is required and has up to 20 characters.
- `id` has up to 256 characters and is not repeated in the same card. Without `id`, the button gets its position in the card, starting at `"1"`.
- A card carries **one** `URL` button, with an `http` or `https` `url`, **or** `REPLY` buttons. The two types do not go in the same card.
- All cards have the same number and type of buttons.

<Warning>
  The `CALL` button and the copy code button (URL `https://www.whatsapp.com/otp/code/...`) are not accepted in the carousel.
</Warning>

## Errors

A carousel outside the rules is refused with `422` and the message is not sent:

| Situation | `error` |
|---|---|
| `CALL` button | `call button is not supported for this channel` |
| Copy code button | `copy button is not supported for this channel` |
| `URL` and `REPLY` in the same card | `URL and REPLY buttons cannot be sent together on this channel` |
| More than one `URL` button in the card | `only one URL button is supported for this channel` |
| Cards with different buttons | `carousel cards must have the same number and type of buttons` |
| Fewer than 2 or more than 10 cards | `carousel must have at least 2 cards` / `carousel must have at most 10 cards` |

The other limits also answer `422`, with the field in the message. For example: `carousel[0].text must be at most 160 characters`.

The request body has up to 10 MiB. Above that, the response is `413`. With many images, send URLs instead of base64.
