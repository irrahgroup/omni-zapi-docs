Removes the reaction from a message. Provide its `messageId`. Any `reaction` sent in the body is ignored.

<Note>
  <ZapiResponseText lang="en" />
</Note>

### Errors

| Status | `error` response | When |
|---|---|---|
| `400` | `phone is required` | `phone` missing or empty |
| `422` | `messageId is required to react to a message` | `messageId` missing or empty |
