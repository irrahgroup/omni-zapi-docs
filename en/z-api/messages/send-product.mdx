<Warning>
  This route **does not send** on Z-API Omni. It always answers `422`. See [Limitations and refusals](/en/z-api/limitations#refused-sends).
</Warning>

On Z-API, this route sends a product from the WhatsApp Business catalog. Sending through this route is not available yet. For product sends through the native API, see [Send product](/en/messages/send-product).

### Errors

| Status | `error` response | When |
|---|---|---|
| `422` | `product message is not available for this channel yet` | Always. Nothing is sent |
