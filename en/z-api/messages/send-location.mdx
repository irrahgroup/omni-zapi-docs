Sends a location. When `latitude` and `longitude` arrive, the message is a location, even if the body has `message` or media: those fields are not sent.

<Note>
  <ZapiResponseText lang="en" />
</Note>

The coordinates are checked **before** authentication. It accepts a JSON body or `application/x-www-form-urlencoded`. A boolean, object or list in `latitude` or `longitude` is also refused with `400`.

### Errors

| Status | `error` response | When |
|---|---|---|
| `400` | `phone is required` | `phone` missing or empty |
| `400` | `latitude and longitude are required` | `latitude` or `longitude` missing or empty |
| `400` | `latitude must be a decimal number between -90 and 90` | `latitude` not in decimal format, out of range or longer than 32 characters |
| `400` | `longitude must be a decimal number between -180 and 180` | `longitude` not in decimal format, out of range or longer than 32 characters |
