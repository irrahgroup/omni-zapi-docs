## Overview

Method that changes the image of an existing group. Send the image as a URL or in Base64.

<Warning>
  On WhatsApp Official, [Meta only accepts](https://developers.facebook.com/documentation/business-messaging/whatsapp/groups/reference#update-group-settings) a square JPEG image, at least 192 x 192 px and up to 5 MB. Anything else is refused by Meta (see the [error codes](https://developers.facebook.com/documentation/business-messaging/whatsapp/groups/error-codes)) and the answer is `422`. `{"value": true}` is Meta's acceptance, not confirmation that the change already appeared in the group.
</Warning>
