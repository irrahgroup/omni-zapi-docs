## Overview

Method that removes one or more administrators from a group.

<Warning>
  On WhatsApp Official this route is not available and answers `422`: the business is the creator and [administrator of the group](https://developers.facebook.com/documentation/business-messaging/whatsapp/groups/reference), and Meta's Groups API has no route to promote or remove administrators.
</Warning>
