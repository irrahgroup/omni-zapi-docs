## Overview

Method that makes the connected number leave a group it takes part in.

<Warning>
  On WhatsApp Official this route is not available and answers `422`. [Meta's Groups API](https://developers.facebook.com/documentation/business-messaging/whatsapp/groups/reference#create-group) has no leave-group action, only deleting the group, which removes all participants, including the business. This route does not do it: the group is not deleted.
</Warning>
