## Overview

Method that returns the group metadata, with all the group information and its participants.

<Warning>
  On WhatsApp Official, Meta does not report several fields of the Z-API format: `owner` is `null` and `isSuperAdmin` is always `false`. The business is the creator and administrator of the group ([Meta](https://developers.facebook.com/documentation/business-messaging/whatsapp/groups/reference)).
</Warning>
