## Overview

Method that removes participants from the group.

<Warning>
  On WhatsApp Official, someone who is removed [cannot return through the invitation link](https://developers.facebook.com/documentation/business-messaging/whatsapp/groups/reference#remove-group-participants-endpoint). Meta [accepts up to 8 participants per request](https://developers.facebook.com/documentation/business-messaging/whatsapp/groups/reference#remove-group-participants-endpoint): with more than 8 numbers, a failure midway can leave some of them already removed. Check the participants in [Group metadata](/en/z-api/groups/group-metadata) before repeating.
</Warning>
