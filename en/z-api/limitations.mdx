## About compatibility

The Z-API compatibility serves **WhatsApp Official** (Meta's official API) and **WhatsApp Web** channels. What a Z-API route does in {projectName} depends on the channel type:

- **WhatsApp Web**: serves most routes, with the same response structure Z-API would give.
- **WhatsApp Official**: Meta's official API applies, which serves some routes and has no equivalent for others.

Channels of other types, such as Instagram, Messenger and Telegram, are not served by the Z-API routes and answer `422`. For those channels, use the [{projectName} message routes](/en/messages/introduction).

A Z-API route that the channel does not serve answers `422` with a message saying the route is not available. A route that does not exist answers `404`, and a wrong HTTP method answers `405`.

The tables below show, per feature, what each channel does:

| Value | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Yes | The route works on the channel. On WhatsApp Web, the response has the Z-API structure |
| Partial | Works with limits, described in the row itself or in the section notice |
| Not yet | The route is not yet released on this channel and answers `422` (rolling out) |
| No | The channel has no equivalent and the route answers `422` |

## Groups

<Info>
  See the [groups introduction](/en/z-api/groups/introduction). On WhatsApp Web, all 19 group routes work as in Z-API. On WhatsApp Official, groups use Meta's Groups API, which serves 12 of these routes, under these conditions:

  - requires a verified Official Business Account (OBA);
  - does not work on a coexistence number (with the WhatsApp Business app on the same number);
  - the group has at most 8 participants;
  - when the group is created, no one is added: people join only through the invite link.

  Without these conditions, or on the 7 routes with no equivalent, the route answers `422`.
</Info>
