## Overview

Method that returns the channel data: ID, token, name, due date, connection, payment status and creation date.

The `due` field is the due date in milliseconds. `name`, `due`, `paymentStatus` and `created` are not returned when the channel has no value.

<Warning>
  The webhook and settings fields that Z-API returns on this route, such as `connectedCallbackUrl`, `callRejectAuto` and `proxyUrl`, are not in the Omni Z-API response.
</Warning>
