## Overview

Method that returns the phone of the channel and whether the account is a business account. It works on WhatsApp Official channels.

<Warning>
  The response has only `phone` and `isBusiness`. Since WhatsApp Official does not use a phone, the Z-API device and profile fields (`imgUrl`, `about`, `name`, `device`, `originalDevice` and `sessionId`) are not returned. `isBusiness` is always `true`, and `phone` is not returned when the channel has no valid phone.
</Warning>
