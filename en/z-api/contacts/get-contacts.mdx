## Overview

Method that returns the WhatsApp contacts of the channel, with pagination: send `page` and `pageSize` in the query.

As in Z-API, the list has the contacts that have a WhatsApp account and also the numbers that are in the same groups as the channel. That is why it usually has more contacts than the phone's address book.
