## Overview

Method that tells whether a number has a WhatsApp account. Send the phone in the path, in the DDI DDD NUMBER format, such as `551199999999`.

Use it to validate numbers, usually in forms. To check several numbers at once, use [Batch check](/en/z-api/contacts/get-iswhatsapp-batch).
