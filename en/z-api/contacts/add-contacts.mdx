## Overview

Method that saves WhatsApp contacts to the phone's contact list. The body is a JSON list, with `firstName` and `phone` required and `lastName` optional in each contact.

As in Z-API, the route only works for WhatsApp accounts that already received the required update, and WhatsApp needs permission, in the app's privacy settings, to add contacts on the phone. Without that, the operation is not completed.
