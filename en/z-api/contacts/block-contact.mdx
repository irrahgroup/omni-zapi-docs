## Overview

Method that blocks or unblocks a contact. Send `phone` and `action`, which is `block` to block or `unblock` to unblock.

`phone` is required and has 8 to 15 digits, numbers only, with country code and area code. A LID, such as `999999999999999@lid`, is not accepted. Outside these rules, the answer is `400` with `phone is required`, `phone must contain only digits, with country and area code` or, for an `action` other than `block` and `unblock`, `action must be block or unblock`.

The success answer is `200` with `{ "value": true }`, including when the contact was already in the requested state.
