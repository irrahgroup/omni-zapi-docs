## Overview

Method that checks in batch whether numbers have a WhatsApp account. It is the batch version of [Number on WhatsApp?](/en/z-api/contacts/get-iswhatsapp): send the numbers in `phones`, in the DDI DDD NUMBER format.

Use it to validate lists, usually in forms. The request body can be up to 10 MiB.
