## Overview

This section documents the 9 Z-API contact routes that {projectName} serves in Z-API Compatibility: query contacts and pictures, check whether a number has WhatsApp, add and remove contacts, report and block. The path (`/instances/` or `/channels/`, followed by `/{id}/token/{token}/` and the route), the body fields and the response format are the Z-API ones. The only change in your application is still the base URL and the authentication header.

The contacts are those of the number connected to WhatsApp Web. The **WhatsApp Official** channel (Meta's official API) does not have these routes and answers `422` to all of them.

## Availability by channel

<ZapiChannelMatrix family="contacts" lang="en" />

- **WhatsApp Web:** all 9 routes are available.
- **WhatsApp Official:** all 9 routes answer `422` with the reason.
- **Other channels** (Telegram, Instagram, Messenger and others): all routes answer `422`.

The `error` texts of these refusals:

| Channel | `error` |
|---|---|
| WhatsApp Official, on the first eight routes of the table | `<route> is not available for META_WHATSAPP channels: <reason>; available only on Z-API (WhatsApp Web) instances`, where `<route>` is the route name without the leading slash, such as `contacts/add` |
| WhatsApp Official, on `POST /contacts/modify-blocked` | `contact route is not available for META_WHATSAPP channels; available only on Z-API (WhatsApp Web) instances` |
| Other channels | `contact route is not available for <TYPE> channels; available only on Z-API (WhatsApp Web) instances`, where `<TYPE>` is the channel type. A channel with no type gets `contact route is not available for this channel type; available only on Z-API (WhatsApp Web) instances` |

## Common errors

All errors have the format `{"error": "<message>"}`. The ones that apply to the contact routes:

| Status | `error` | When |
|---|---|---|
| `422` | `instance subscription is not active` | The channel subscription is not active. |
| `400` | `invalid path parameter` | Phone in the path is empty, longer than 128 characters or has an invalid character. |
| `400` | `request body is too large` | The body is over the route limit: 10 MiB on `phone-exists-batch` and 1 MiB on the other routes with a body. |
