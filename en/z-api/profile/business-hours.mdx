## Overview

Method to update the company business hours in the WhatsApp Business profile. Send `timezone` and, optionally, `mode` (`specificHours`, `open24h` or `appointmentOnly`) and `days`, with `dayOfWeek`, `openTime` and `closeTime` for each day.

It works on **WhatsApp Web** channels.
