## Overview

Method to update the company websites in the WhatsApp Business profile. Send the URLs in `websites`, at most 2. On WhatsApp Web, an empty list removes the websites, as in Z-API.

<Warning>
  On Official WhatsApp, each website has at most 256 characters and must start with `http://` or `https://`, [Meta limits](https://developers.facebook.com/docs/whatsapp/cloud-api/reference/business-profiles). If Meta refuses the value, the response is `422` with `the channel provider refused the request`.
</Warning>
