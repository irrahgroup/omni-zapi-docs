## Overview

Method to update the company address in the WhatsApp Business profile. Send the address in `value`. On WhatsApp Web, an empty `value` removes the address, as in Z-API.

<Warning>
  On Official WhatsApp, the address has at most 256 characters, a [Meta limit](https://developers.facebook.com/docs/whatsapp/cloud-api/reference/business-profiles). Above that, the response is `400` with `address must have at most 256 characters`.
</Warning>
