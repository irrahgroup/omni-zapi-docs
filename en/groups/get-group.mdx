## Overview

A group's data.

<Note>
  A group that does not exist responds `404` `provider_error`; when the channel is no longer a member of the group (it left or was removed), the response is `404` `{"error":"provider_error","message":"GROUP_FORBIDDEN"}`, and a temporary lookup failure at the provider can return the same error for up to 5 minutes; a channel that does not exist responds `404` `instance not found`; an id out of format responds `400` `invalid_request`.
</Note>
