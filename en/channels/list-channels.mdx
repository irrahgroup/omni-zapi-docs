## Overview

Returns the channels in the workspace of your Secret Key, newest first. Deleted channels are not listed, and neither are channels from other workspaces — the scope is always the account that owns the key.

<Warning>
  <EnterpriseText lang="en" />
</Warning>

### Pagination

| Parameter | Default | Limit |
|---|---|---|
| `page` | `0` | — |
| `pageSize` | `20` | `100` |

The response carries `total` (channels matching the filter), `totalPage`, `pageSize` and `page`, with the channels in `content`.

### Filters

| Parameter | Effect |
|---|---|
| `query` | Channel name or identifier, case-insensitive |
| `type` | Channel technology: `META_WHATSAPP`, `Z_API_WHATSAPP`, `META_INSTAGRAM`, `META_MESSENGER`, `BOT_TELEGRAM` |
| `middleware` | Alias of `type`. If both are sent, `type` wins |

<Note>
  In Z-API, `middleware` tells whether the instance runs on `web` or `mobile`. Omni has no such distinction: the field holds the channel technology, the same value accepted in [Create channel](/en/channels/create-channel). The `middleware` parameter exists for those who migrated from Z-API and kept the old name in their code.
</Note>

### Channel webhooks

Each channel carries `webhooksCount`, the number of webhook endpoints registered on it. To see the URLs and events of each one, use [Get channel](/en/channels/get-channel) or [List webhooks](/en/webhooks/list-webhooks).
