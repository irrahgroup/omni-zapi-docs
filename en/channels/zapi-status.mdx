## Overview

Tells whether a `Z_API_WHATSAPP` (WhatsApp Web) channel is connected. Use it after the user scans the [QR Code](/en/channels/zapi-qr-code), to confirm the connection, and whenever you need to know whether the channel is up.

Use the Secret Key, from your backend. Does not require ENTERPRISE.

```json
{"connected": true, "session": true, "created": 1762790400000, "error": null, "smartphoneConnected": true}
```

| Field | What it tells |
|---|---|
| `connected` | `true` when the number is connected to the channel |
| `smartphoneConnected` | `true` when the number's phone is online |
| `session`, `created`, `error` | Informative |

With `connected: false`, generate a new QR Code in [Get QR Code](/en/channels/zapi-qr-code) for the user to scan.

### Errors

| Status | Body | When |
|---|---|---|
| `400` | `{"error":"Authorization invalid"}` | Secret Key missing, malformed or unknown |
| `400` | `{"error":"203.0.113.10 not allowed"}` | IP outside the [IP list](/en/security/ip-restriction) |
| `404` | `{"error":"Instance not found"}` | Channel does not exist or belongs to another workspace |
| `502` | `{"error":"integration_unavailable", ...}` | Integration failure; try again in a few moments |

<Note>
  The `channelId` comes from the [Create channel](/en/channels/create-channel) endpoint or from [List channels](/en/channels/list-channels).
</Note>
