## Overview

Disconnects a `META_MESSENGER` channel and clears the link to the account, page or bot. No body.

Use it to switch to another Facebook page, or solve the `422` from [List Messenger pages](/en/channels/list-messenger-pages), which shows up when the channel is still active on the provider.

Use the Secret Key, from your backend. Does not require ENTERPRISE.

```bash
curl -X POST https://api.omni.z-api.io/v1/channels/CHANNEL_ID/deactivate-messenger \
  -H "Authorization: Bearer YOUR_SECRET_KEY"
```

The response is a boolean. With `true`, the channel is disconnected and the link is cleared. With `false`, nothing changes on the channel.

To connect again, redo the connection through the SDK and finish with [Activate Messenger](/en/channels/activate-messenger). The equivalent routes of the other social channels are [Deactivate Instagram](/en/channels/deactivate-instagram), [Deactivate Messenger](/en/channels/deactivate-messenger) and [Deactivate Telegram](/en/channels/deactivate-telegram).

### Errors

| Status | Body | When |
|---|---|---|
| `400` | `{"error":"Authorization invalid"}` | Secret Key missing, malformed or unknown |
| `400` | `{"error":"203.0.113.10 not allowed"}` | IP outside the [IP list](/en/security/ip-restriction) |
| `404` | `{"error":"Instance not found"}` | Channel does not exist or belongs to another workspace |
| `502` | `{"error":"integration_unavailable", ...}` | Integration failure; try again in a few moments |

<Note>
  The `channelId` comes from the [Create channel](/en/channels/create-channel) endpoint or from [List channels](/en/channels/list-channels).
</Note>
