## How does it work?

When a customer sends a message or calls your number, Meta opens a **24-hour customer service window**.

Each new message or call from the customer **resets** the timer to 24 hours.

<Note>
  The window defines **what you can send**, not whether the message is free. The cost of each message depends on its **category**. See [Billing](/en/whatsapp-official/billing).
</Note>

## Inside the window (24h)

You can send **free-form messages** (text, media, audio, documents, buttons, lists) using the [free messages](/en/messages/introduction) endpoints, with no template needed.

These messages belong to the **Service** category:

- Each business phone number gets **1,000 free service messages per month**.
- Starting with the **1,001st**, each delivered message is charged.

<Note>
  Eligible government and non-profit organizations follow a separate rule. See [Billing](/en/whatsapp-official/billing).
</Note>

<Warning>
  You can also send **templates** while the window is open, but they are **charged by template category** (Marketing, Utility, or Authentication), even inside the window. The exception is the [free entry point window](#free-entry-via-ads).
</Warning>

## Outside the window

When the window is closed, the only way to message the customer is through **Meta-approved templates** using the [Send template](/en/messages/send-template) endpoint.

| Category | When to use | Cost |
|---|---|---|
| **Marketing** | Promotions, campaigns, offers | Charged per message |
| **Utility** | Confirmations, order updates, alerts | Charged per message |
| **Authentication** | OTP code, login verification | Charged per message |

<Warning>
  Free-form messages sent outside the window are **rejected by the API**. Also remember to only message customers who have **opted in** to receive messages from your business.
</Warning>

## How to reopen the window?

There are two ways:

<Steps>
  <Step title="The customer sends a message or calls">
    A new 24h window opens immediately.
  </Step>
  <Step title="You send a template and the customer replies">
    Send an [approved template](/en/messages/send-template). When the customer **replies**, their reply opens the 24h window and you can send [free messages](/en/messages/introduction) again. The template itself is charged by category, except in the free entry point window.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Free entry via ads

When the customer comes through a **Click-to-WhatsApp (CTWA)** ad using the WhatsApp app on **Android or iOS**, a **free entry point window** can open.

<Note>
  Access through WhatsApp **Desktop** or **Web** does not count toward the free entry point window.
</Note>

<Steps>
  <Step title="The customer sends a message from the ad">
    Their message opens the regular **24h** customer service window.
  </Step>
  <Step title="You reply within 24h">
    Your reply (Marketing, Utility, Authentication, or Service) is **free** and opens the **free entry point window**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Free entry point window open for up to 7 days">
    During this period, **Marketing**, **Utility**, **Authentication**, and **Service** messages are **free**. 7 days is the maximum, not a guarantee: Meta documents that the window **may** remain open for up to 7 days. See [24-hour window](/en/whatsapp-official/24h-window).
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Warning>
  The free entry point window changes **the cost**, not **the sending rule**. Free-form messages still require the 24h window to be open. Once it closes, send a template, which is free while the free entry point window is open.
</Warning>

<Warning>
  **Meta Business Agent** messages are still **charged** in the free entry point window.
</Warning>

<Info>
  The free entry point window is **shared across all accounts** sending from the same business phone number, not only the account that paid for the ad.
</Info>

## Visual summary

| Situation | Can send free-form messages? | Template required? | Cost |
|---|---|---|---|
| Customer messaged in the last 24h | Yes | No | Service: 1,000 free/month per number, then charged |
| Template sent while the window is open | — | Yes | Charged by template category |
| Window closed | No | Yes | Charged by template category |
| Free entry point window open (came from a CTWA ad and you replied within 24h) | Yes, while the 24h window is open | Yes, after the 24h window closes | Free for up to 7 days (except Meta Business Agent) |
