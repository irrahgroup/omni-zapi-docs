## Overview

Returns the list of WABAs (WhatsApp Business Accounts) linked to your token. The returned `businessId` is required for all other template endpoints.

Each WABA carries `templatesStatus`. `AVAILABLE` means its templates answered in time. `UNAVAILABLE` means they did not answer within the list deadline: the WABA stays in the list, and you can query its templates again in a few moments.
