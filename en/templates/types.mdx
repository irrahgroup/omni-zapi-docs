The type defines **which fields exist** in the template and what Meta requires for each. Picking the type is the first decision, and it does not change later: to switch, you create another template.

<Note>
  The previews below approximate how the customer sees it in WhatsApp. Anything in `{{braces}}` is a variable, filled in at send time.
</Note>

## Approval and sending: two different things

Several types are **approved once and fed at send time**. The template holds the structure; the data goes in when you fire it.

| Approved at creation | Sent on every dispatch |
|---|---|
| text, buttons, card structure | variable values |
| category and language | products, order items, card media |
| the media sample Meta reviews | the real media of each send |

That is what lets you use **the same approved template across different campaigns**.

## Text limits

| Field | Limit |
|---|---|
| Header | 60 characters, at most **one** variable |
| Body | 1024 characters |
| Footer | 60 characters, **no** variables |
| Button text | 25 characters |
| Carousel card text | 160 characters |
| Offer text | 16 characters |
| Coupon code | 20 characters |

## Message

### Custom message

`custom`

Free text with variables, a header and buttons. It is the most used type and the only fully open one.

<TemplatePreview
  header="Order {{order_number}} confirmed"
  body="Hi {{customer_name}}, your order {{order_number}} is confirmed and ships today by 6pm."
  footer="Reply STOP to opt out"
  buttons={['Track order', 'See details']}
/>

## Authentication

### Access code

`authentication`

OTP delivery. **Meta defines the text** — you do not write the body, you only configure the copy button.

<TemplatePreview
  body="<your code> is your verification code. For your security, do not share it."
  buttons={['Copy code']}
  caption="The text is fixed by Meta. So is the category: always AUTHENTICATION." 
/>

## Commerce

### Catalog

`catalog`

Opens the full store catalog inside WhatsApp.

<Info>
  **Data at send time** — The top image comes from the catalog. If you do not pick a product, Meta uses the first one in the account.
</Info>

<TemplatePreview
  headerKind="catalog image"
  body="New arrivals in the store. Tap the button below to see the full catalog."
  footer="Prices valid while stock lasts"
  buttons={['View catalog']}
/>

### Multi-product

`mpm`

A showcase with sections and several products.

<Info>
  **Data at send time** — The products (**up to 30, across up to 10 sections**) and the top image. The same showcase serves different campaigns.
</Info>

<TemplatePreview
  header="This week's picks"
  body="We picked a few products that go with what you bought. See the full list."
  buttons={['View items']}
/>

### Single product

`spm`

One catalog product with photo, name and price, plus the "View product" button.

<Info>
  **Data at send time** — The product, with photo, name and price, comes from the catalog.
</Info>

<TemplatePreview
  headerKind="product photo"
  body="This is the product you asked about. Tap to see the details and buy."
  footer="Limited stock"
  buttons={['View product']}
  caption="The button text is fixed by Meta." 
/>

### Checkout button

`checkout_button`

Takes the customer straight to payment.

<Info>
  **Data at send time** — The order: items, subtotal, total and the payment method. It changes on every purchase.
</Info>

<TemplatePreview
  body="Your cart is ready: {{item_count}} items, total {{total_amount}}. Review and check out here."
  buttons={['Check out', 'I need help']}
/>

### Order details

`order_details`

The order invoice with items and total, plus the button that opens payment by Pix or link.

<Info>
  **Data at send time** — The items, the subtotal, the total and the Pix code. They change on every order.
</Info>

<TemplatePreview
  header="Order {{order_number}}"
  body="Your order is ready! Check the details below and complete the payment."
  footer="Payment processed by WhatsApp"
  buttons={['Pay now']}
/>

## Promotion

### Coupon

`coupon_code`

A message with a copyable discount code. The code is up to **20 characters**.

<TemplatePreview
  body="{{customer_name}}, you got a 10% coupon for your next purchase. Tap to copy the code."
  coupon="COMEBACK10"
  buttons={['Copy code', 'Use coupon']}
/>

### Limited time offer

`limited_time_offer`

A promotion with an expiration counter. The offer text is up to **16 characters**.

<TemplatePreview
  offer="20% off"
  body="{{customer_name}}, your offer ends in a few hours. Use the coupon before it expires."
  buttons={['Grab it now']}
  caption="WhatsApp shows a live counter until expiration." 
/>

## Carousel

### Media carousel

`media_card_carousel`

Up to **10 cards** with an image or a video. Each card body is up to 160 characters.

<Info>
  **Data at send time** — The media you set at creation is only the **sample Meta reviews**. At send time, each card image or video goes as a parameter and can be a different one: the same approved carousel serves different campaigns.
</Info>

<TemplatePreview
  body="The new collection is here. Swipe to see the highlights."
  cards={[{ text: 'Floral midi dress', button: 'I want this' }, { text: 'Tailored blazer', button: 'I want this' }]}
/>

### Product carousel

`product_card_carousel`

Cards pulled from your catalog.

<Info>
  **Data at send time** — The photo, the name and the price of each card come from the catalog.
</Info>

<TemplatePreview
  body="We picked a few products for you. Swipe to see them."
  cards={[{ media: 'catalog product', text: 'Basic tee', button: 'View product' }, { media: 'catalog product', text: 'Chino trousers', button: 'View product' }]}
  caption="Created with exactly 2 cards, but can send up to 10." 
/>

## Other

### Call permission

`call_permission_request`

Asks for permission to call through WhatsApp.

<TemplatePreview
  body="Can we call you about your order {{order_number}}?"
  buttons={['Allow', 'No, thanks']}
/>

### Template Library

`library`

A ready-made model from Meta's library. **It skips review** — approved from the start.

<TemplatePreview
  body="Hi {{customer_name}}, your order {{order_number}} has shipped and arrives in {{eta}}."
  buttons={['Track']}
  caption="The content comes from Meta's library and you do not edit the text." 
/>
