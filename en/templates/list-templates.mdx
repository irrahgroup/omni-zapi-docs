## Overview

Returns all templates in the WABA as `{"data": [...]}`. The `components` structure returned varies according to each template's category and configuration.

The list comes from the platform snapshot, the copy of the templates that the sync with Meta keeps. To fetch the current state from Meta, use [Sync templates](/en/templates/sync-templates).

<Warning>
  This route has no filter or pagination. Query string parameters, such as `limit` or `status`, are ignored, and the response brings every template at once. To filter, filter the list on your side.
</Warning>

### Empty snapshot

When the WABA snapshot is empty, the listing syncs with Meta before responding. In that case, the response also brings `synced`, `syncedComplete` and `syncedCount`, with the same meaning as in [Sync templates](/en/templates/sync-templates).

<Note>
  The `wabaId` is obtained from the [List WABAs](/en/templates/list-businesses) endpoint.
</Note>
