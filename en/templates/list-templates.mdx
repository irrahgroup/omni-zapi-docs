## Overview

Returns all templates registered in a WABA. The `components` structure returned varies according to each template's category and configuration.

The list comes complete, in a single response: there is no pagination and no status filter. To see only approved templates, for example, filter on your side by the `status` field.

The list comes from the platform's records, which are updated by Meta's status events and by [Sync templates](/en/templates/sync-templates). A template created directly at Meta only shows up after a sync.

For a single template, use [Get template](/en/templates/get-template).

<Note>
  The `wabaId` is obtained from the [List WABAs](/en/templates/list-businesses) endpoint.
</Note>
