description: "The call that creates the template: route, authentication, responses and errors"
openapi: "/en/templates/openapi.json POST /whatsapp/businesses/{wabaId}/templates"
---

This is the single creation call. **Its body is the JSON of the model** you picked — there is no separate route per model, and sending the payload from [Models](/en/templates/models/overview) already creates the template.

What lives here is the mechanics and the return: the route, the `wabaId`, the schema field by field and what comes back in each case. The playground above fires the real call.

The template starts as `PENDING` and becomes `APPROVED` or `REJECTED` after Meta's review. Use [Sync templates](/en/templates/sync-templates) to force a status update.

<Note>
  The `wabaId` comes from the [List WABAs](/en/templates/list-businesses) endpoint.
</Note>

<Warning>
  The name goes in `snake_case`, with no spaces or special characters, and **cannot be changed** after creation.
</Warning>

<Warning>
  This endpoint is scoped by **WABA**, so today it covers only **Official WhatsApp**.

  Instagram and Messenger also have a pre-approved template — the **Utility Template**, scoped by page. Support for them in Omni Z-API is **being implemented**. See the [comparison of the three template kinds](/en/templates/introduction).
</Warning>
