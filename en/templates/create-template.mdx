This is the single creation call. **Its body is the JSON of the model** you picked — there is no separate route per model, and sending the payload from [Models](/en/templates/models/overview) already creates the template.

What lives here is the mechanics and the return: the route, the `wabaId`, the schema field by field and what comes back in each case. The playground above fires the real call.

The template starts as `PENDING` and becomes `APPROVED` or `REJECTED` after Meta's review. The preferred way to learn the result is the [template webhook](/en/webhooks/template-webhooks), which does not depend on a channel, subscribed to the `UPDATE_TEMPLATE_STATUS` event: the platform notifies you when Meta decides. To check a specific template, use [Get template](/en/templates/get-template) with the `id` returned here. If the status looks outdated, [Sync templates](/en/templates/sync-templates) forces an update with Meta.

Meta rejects the template with `422`, and the body carries the original refusal in `error.errors[]`. The `400` errors come only from the platform: authentication refused, missing body or empty template.

<Note>
  The `wabaId` comes from the [List WABAs](/en/templates/list-businesses) endpoint.
</Note>

<Warning>
  The name goes in `snake_case`, with no spaces or special characters, and **cannot be changed** after creation.
</Warning>
