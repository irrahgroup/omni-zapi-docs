## Overview

Registers a click on an option.

<Note>
  `type`: `BUTTON`, `LIST`, or `TEMPLATE_BUTTON`. Responds `204`, no body.
</Note>

<Note>
  The `204` confirms that the request was accepted by WhatsApp Web, not that the option exists. The API does not check whether the `optionId` belongs to the message or whether the message is interactive: a nonexistent option also responds `204`, and nothing reaches the contact.
</Note>
