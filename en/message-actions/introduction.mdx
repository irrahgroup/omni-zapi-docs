## Overview

Actions on a message already sent or received, in the new API, under `/v1/channels/{channelId}/messages/...`. **On the WhatsApp Web channel** (`Z_API_WHATSAPP`): delete, forward, pin/unpin, and choose option.

The `messageId` is the **Omni id** — the same one returned on send (`POST /messages`) and in the webhook.

<Note>
  On a Meta channel (`META_WHATSAPP`), each action responds `422` with a `subCode` (for example `DELETE_NOT_SUPPORTED`). The [capabilities](/en/message-actions/capabilities) route responds `200` with an empty `operations`.
</Note>

<Note>
  A disconnected channel responds `422` `CHANNEL_DISCONNECTED`, with the message of what was not done. A message that does not exist responds `404` `MESSAGE_NOT_FOUND`.
</Note>
