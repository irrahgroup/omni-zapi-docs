## Overview

Actions on a message already sent or received, in the new API, under `/v1/channels/{channelId}/messages/...`. **On the WhatsApp Web channel** (`Z_API_WHATSAPP`): delete, forward, pin/unpin, and choose option.

The `messageId` is the **Omni id** — the same one returned on send (`POST /messages`) and in the webhook.

<Note>
  On a Meta channel (`META_WHATSAPP`), each action responds `422` with a `subCode` (for example `DELETE_NOT_SUPPORTED`). The [capabilities](/en/message-actions/capabilities) route responds `200` with an empty `operations`.
</Note>

<Note>
  A disconnected channel is checked **before** the message: it responds `422` `CHANNEL_DISCONNECTED`, with the message of what was not done. The `404` `MESSAGE_NOT_FOUND` only appears with the channel connected. Delete and forward may also respond `422` `INSTANCE_NOT_PROVISIONED`, `MESSAGE_WITHOUT_PROVIDER_ID` or `CHAT_NOT_SUPPORTED` (by chat type).
</Note>

## Common errors

Beyond the above, every message action may respond, on the **Omni API**: `401` when the credential is refused; `403` `ip_not_allowed` when the IP is off the list; `404` `{"error": "instance not found"}` when the channel does not exist; `422` when the instance is not provisioned, the subscription is inactive or the workspace is blocked; and `502` when barling fails.
