<Warning>
  **Being implemented.** Only **one contact per message** works today: `attachments` accepts a list, but nothing from the second item on is sent.

  We also carry only `name` and `phones`. [Meta accepts](https://developers.facebook.com/documentation/business-messaging/whatsapp/messages/contacts-messages) up to **257 contacts** in one send, and on each one also email, address, organization, birthday and website.

  We are extending the endpoint. Until then, send one contact per request.
</Warning>

## Overview

Sends a contact to a recipient. The `content.type` field must be `CONTACT`. You can send multiple phone numbers in the `phones` array.
