## Overview

Returns a delivery with the same fields as the [list](/en/webhooks/list-deliveries) and every attempt, in `attempts`.

The `deliveryId` is the UUID in the `x-webhook-delivery-id` header, or the `deliveryId` of a list item.

### What each attempt has

- The attempt number and the origin: `AUTOMATIC` (an attempt by {projectName}), `MANUAL_RESEND` (a resend you requested) or `SYSTEM`.
- The time, the outcome, the HTTP status, the latency and the error class.
- The URL and the headers sent, with sensitive parts hidden.
- The body sent and the body of your endpoint's response. A body that is too large is cut, and `requestBodyTruncated` or `responseBodyTruncated` is `true`.

### Hidden bodies

With the workspace primary key, the bodies come in the response. When they do not, `bodyHidden` is `true` and the body fields are `null`. This happens:

- with an API key without `webhooks:write`;
- on a billing or platform event delivery, with an API key without `billing:read`;
- on deliveries to a connected app (`source: "APP"`), which always come without URL, headers or bodies.

See [API key with permissions](/en/webhooks/deliveries#api-key-with-permissions).

### Errors

| Status | When |
|---|---|
| `400` | `deliveryId` is not a UUID |
| `403` | The API key does not have `webhooks:read`. See [API key with permissions](/en/webhooks/deliveries#api-key-with-permissions) |
| `404` | The delivery does not exist, is older than the period your plan keeps, or belongs to another workspace |
| `503` | The history took more than 5 seconds or is down. Try again in a moment |
