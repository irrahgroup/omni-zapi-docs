## Overview

The webhook center shows every event delivery to your webhooks: the attempts, the status, the latency and your server's response. With it you can:

- query deliveries, filtered by webhook, status, event type and period;
- see a delivery's detail, with the request sent and the response received on each attempt;
- resend one delivery, or in bulk the deliveries that failed in a failure episode;
- see the state of each webhook in the workspace (channel, default and global);
- reactivate a suspended webhook.

## Authentication and access

Every route uses the workspace Secret Key, like the other API routes:

```
Authorization: Bearer <secret-key>
```

- The `Bearer` prefix is optional.
- Any workspace role can use the center. The `ENTERPRISE` plan is not required.
- The workspace comes from the Secret Key itself. There is no parameter to query another workspace: a delivery or webhook from another workspace returns `404`, the same as an id that does not exist.
- Every route also exists without the `/v1` prefix (for example, `GET /webhooks/deliveries`).

## History by format

| Webhook format | History in the center |
|---|---|
| `DEFAULT` | Yes. Deliveries appear in the center and can be resent. |
| `Z_API` | Yes. The resend uses the stored body, in the same format. |
| `CHATWOOT` | No. The webhook appears in the health with `state` `NO_HISTORY`, with no deliveries, notices or suspension. |

## Retention

The center keeps deliveries for the retention period of the workspace plan:

| Workspace role | Retention |
|---|---|
| `ENTERPRISE` | 3 days |
| Others | 1 day |

The current retention comes in the `retentionDays` field of [Webhook health](/en/webhooks/webhooks-health). Queries only show what is within the **current** retention: when moving from `ENTERPRISE` to another role, anything older than 1 day disappears from queries at once.

## Delivery statuses

| `status` | Meaning |
|---|---|
| `SUCCEEDED` | Your server responded `2xx`. |
| `RETRYING` | It failed and will be retried at `nextAttemptAt`. |
| `FAILED` | It failed and will not be retried. |
| `NOT_SENT` | Nothing was sent. `notSentReason` gives the reason: `SUSPENDED` (webhook suspended) or `CIRCUIT_OPEN` (the destination stayed unavailable until the deadline). |
| `EXPIRED` | 23 hours passed without success. |
| `UNKNOWN` | The next attempt should have happened more than 1 hour ago and there is no record of it. |
| `RESENT` | Resent less than 1 hour ago, waiting for the result. |

## When a delivery is retried

| Your server's response | What happens |
|---|---|
| `2xx` | Delivered. |
| `408`, `429`, `5xx`, no response within 5 seconds, or a connection error | Retry, up to 7 attempts within 23 hours. |
| Any other `4xx`, or `3xx` | No retry: the delivery ends as `FAILED`. Fix the problem and use the resend. |

The full attempt schedule is in [Delivery and retries](/en/webhooks/delivery).

## How to recover failed deliveries

<Steps>
  <Step title="Check the webhook health">
    Call [Webhook health](/en/webhooks/webhooks-health). A webhook with `state` `FAILING` is failing; `consecutiveFailures`, `failingSince` and `lastFailureAt` show since when.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Find the cause">
    [List the deliveries](/en/webhooks/list-deliveries) of the webhook with `status=FAILED` and open one in [Get delivery](/en/webhooks/get-delivery). `httpStatus`, `errorMessage` and `responseBody` show what your server responded.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Fix your server">
    Fix the endpoint, the URL or the webhook authentication. The resend uses the webhook's **current** URL, authentication and secret.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Resend">
    For one delivery, use [Resend delivery](/en/webhooks/resend-delivery). For every delivery that failed in the episode, use [Resend deliveries in bulk](/en/webhooks/resend-deliveries) and repeat every 2 minutes while `remaining` is greater than zero, before `oldestExpiresAt`.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  A resent delivery arrives with the **same** `x-webhook-delivery-id` and a greater `x-webhook-attempt`. If you deduplicate by `x-webhook-delivery-id`, accept an already seen delivery when `x-webhook-attempt` is greater than the last one processed. See [Delivery and retries](/en/webhooks/delivery#deduplicate-by-x-webhook-delivery-id).
</Note>

## Failure notices

When a webhook fails repeatedly, the workspace Owner and Managers receive email notices, and the notice shows up in the dashboard bell:

| Notice | When it is sent |
|---|---|
| 1st | After at least 3 consecutive failures, spanning at least 1 hour |
| 2nd | At least 1 hour after the 1st, with 3 new failures |
| 3rd | At least 6 hours after the 2nd, with 3 new failures |

A `2xx` response ends the sequence.

**Where automatic suspension is on for the workspace brand, delivery is disabled together with the 3rd notice.** Where it is off, the notices do not disable the webhook: it stays in `state` `FAILING` and keeps receiving deliveries, with the usual retries.

<Note>
  While delivery is suspended, new events keep being recorded, without being sent, for the plan retention, with `status` `NOT_SENT` and `notSentReason` `SUSPENDED`. Changing the webhook URL or status does not reactivate it: use [Reactivate webhook](/en/webhooks/reactivate-webhook).
</Note>

## Errors common to every route

| Case | Response |
|---|---|
| `Authorization` missing or not a valid Secret Key | `400` with `{"error": "Authorization invalid"}` |
| Call from an IP outside the workspace IP allowlist | `400` with `{"error": "203.0.113.10 not allowed"}` |
| Workspace without an active configuration | `422` with `{"error": "Webhook management requires an active workspace"}` |
| The operation could not be completed right now | `502` with `{"error": "integration_unavailable", "message": "The operation could not be completed right now. Try again in a few moments."}` |

Validation and business-rule errors (`400`, `404`, `422` and `429`) arrive as `{"error": {"message": "..."}}`. The resend `422` also carries `type` and `details.reason`. The `503` arrives as `{"error": "<message>"}`.
