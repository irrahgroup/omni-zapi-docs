## Overview

Reactivates a suspended webhook: new events are sent to it again. The route has no body.

The `200` response has the webhook state, with the same fields as each item in [webhook health](/en/webhooks/webhooks-health).

If the webhook is not suspended, nothing changes: the response has the current state.

It works for channel, default and template webhooks, by `webhookId`.

<Tip>
  Reactivation does not resend what failed before. For that, use [Resend failed deliveries](/en/webhooks/resend-deliveries) after you reactivate.
</Tip>

### Errors

| Status | When |
|---|---|
| `404` | The webhook does not exist or belongs to another workspace |
