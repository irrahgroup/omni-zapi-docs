## How does billing work?

Since **July 1, 2025**, Meta charges **per delivered message** (no longer per conversation). Only messages **from the business to the customer** are charged; customer messages are never charged.

The price depends on:

- The message **category**
- The **market**: the country calling code of the recipient's number (e.g., `+55` = Brazil)
- **Monthly volume**, for Utility and Authentication only (see [Volume tiers](#volume-tiers-utility-and-authentication))

## The 5 categories

| Category | What it is | How it's charged |
|---|---|---|
| **Marketing** `MARKETING` | Promotional template | Fixed price per message, according to Meta's rate card |
| **Utility** `UTILITY` | Transactional or informational template | Fixed price per message, with volume tier discounts |
| **Authentication** `AUTHENTICATION` | OTP code template | Fixed price per message, with volume tier discounts; some markets have an authentication-international rate |
| **Service** | Free-form message inside the 24h window (human agent, chatbot, or third-party AI) | 1,000 free per month per business phone number; then a fixed price per message |
| **Meta Business Agent** | Free-form message generated by Meta's AI agent (since July 1, 2026) | US$ 2.00 per 1 million tokens, single global rate |

<Note>
  Each message has **a single category** and generates **a single charge**. See the price of each template category in Brazil in [Message templates](/en/whatsapp-official/message-template).
</Note>

<Info>
  For the **Meta Business Agent**, both input and output tokens are charged. According to Meta, a message typically uses **20,000 to 25,000 tokens**, roughly **4 to 5 US cents** per message. This category has no volume tiers and no free messages.
</Info>

## What is not charged

- Messages from the customer to the business
- The first **1,000 service messages per month, per business phone number**. Unused messages don't roll over, and the count resets at midnight on the 1st, in the account's time zone
- All Marketing, Utility, Authentication, and Service messages in the **free entry point window** (customer coming from a Click-to-WhatsApp ad, up to 7 days), except Meta Business Agent. See [Conversation window](/en/conversation-window)
- Messages that were not delivered

## What changed on October 1, 2026

- **Service is charged again** (it had been free since November 1, 2024), with a free tier of 1,000 messages/month per business phone number
- **Utility inside the 24h window is charged again** (it had been free since July 1, 2025)
- Without a [payment method](/en/whatsapp-official/payment) on file, Meta only delivers service messages within the free tier; after that, they stop being delivered

<Info>
  The fields below belong to the **Meta Cloud API status webhook**, not to the {projectName} webhook — see the [`MESSAGE_STATUS`](/en/webhooks/channel-webhooks) event. In Meta's webhook, the `pricing.type` field shows how the message was handled:

  | Value | Meaning |
  |---|---|
  | `regular` | Charged message |
  | `free_customer_service` | Free Service message, within the number's free tier of 1,000 messages/month |
  | `free_entry_point` | Free message sent in the free entry point window (`pricing.category` is `referral_conversion`) |
  | `free_group_customer_service` | Free group Service message, within the number's free tier |

  Through September 30, 2026, `free_customer_service` indicated any Utility or free-form message sent inside the customer service window. Since October 1, 2026, being inside the window **no longer makes a message free**: Service messages beyond the free tier and Utility messages inside the window arrive as `regular`.
</Info>

<Note>
  **Eligible government and non-profit organizations** keep free service messages beyond the 1,000 until **December 31, 2027**. Eligibility is shown in the `service_pricing_policy` field (`PAID_EXEMPT`, `REGULAR`, or `UNKNOWN`), and these messages arrive in the webhook with `pricing.subtype` set to `paid_exempt`.
</Note>

<Note>
  The October 1, 2026 service message changes **do not apply to AI Providers**, who follow Meta's [AI Providers pricing policy](https://developers.facebook.com/documentation/business-messaging/whatsapp/pricing/ai-providers).
</Note>

## Volume tiers (Utility and Authentication)

The more **Utility** or **Authentication** messages the portfolio sends in a month to the same market, the **lower the rate** for subsequent messages.

- The count adds up **all accounts in the portfolio**, per market–category pair
- Only **charged** messages count; free messages (free entry point window) are not included
- The count **resets every month**, at midnight in the account's time zone
- Tier changes are notified through **Meta's** `account_update` webhook, with `event` set to `VOLUME_BASED_PRICING_TIER_UPDATE`

## Brazil: billing in BRL

<Steps>
  <Step title="Since July 1, 2026">
    BRL-billed accounts started rolling out to eligible businesses in Brazil. Since July 16, 2026, all eligible businesses can create BRL accounts.
  </Step>
  <Step title="By June 30, 2027">
    All accounts of these customers must **migrate to BRL**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Starting July 1, 2027">
    Meta **stops delivering** messages from accounts in other currencies.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## When prices change

Meta only changes rates on the **1st day of each quarter** (Jan 1, Apr 1, Jul 1, Oct 1), with minimum notice of:

| Type of change | Minimum notice |
|---|---|
| Rate card update (including volume tiers) | 1 month |
| New pricing model features (e.g., new volume tiers) | 3 months |
| Pricing model change | 6 months |

Check the current rates on [Meta's pricing page](https://developers.facebook.com/documentation/business-messaging/whatsapp/pricing).
