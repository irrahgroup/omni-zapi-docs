## What is a template?

A **template** (message template) is a message registered and **approved by Meta**. It can be sent at any time and is the **only way** to start or resume a conversation when the [24h window](/en/conversation-window) is closed.

A template can include text, images, video, documents, buttons, and variables.

<Warning>
  Every delivered template is **charged by its category**, inside or outside the 24h window. The only exception is the **free entry point window** (Click-to-WhatsApp ads). See [Billing](/en/whatsapp-official/billing).
</Warning>

## What happens after sending a template?

| Situation | Result |
|---|---|
| The customer **replies** | A new 24h window opens and you can send [free messages](/en/messages/introduction) again |
| The customer **doesn't reply** | The window stays closed; only another template can be sent |

## Categories

<Note>
  The code name is the value of the `category` field in the API. See [Templates](/en/templates/introduction) to create and manage yours.
</Note>

| Category | Purpose | Examples | Key rules |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Marketing** `MARKETING` | Promote, sell, re-engage | Coupon, abandoned cart, subscription renewal, birthday, app download invite | Any template with mixed content (utility + promotion) or vague content, such as only `{{1}}` or "Congratulations!", is also Marketing |
| **Utility** `UTILITY` | Respond to a customer action or request | Order confirmation, tracking, invoice, payment reminder, survey about a specific interaction | No promotional tone at all, and either specific to the customer (order, account, service) or essential (safety, public alert) |
| **Authentication** `AUTHENTICATION` | Verify identity with a one-time passcode (OTP) | "123456 is your verification code" | Fixed text from the Template Library, no customization; copy code or one-tap button, or zero-tap (Android only); no URLs, media, or emojis; variables up to 15 characters |

<Note>
  Authentication templates can optionally include a security disclaimer ("For your security, do not share this code") and a code expiration warning.
</Note>

<Note>
  Free-form messages sent inside the window **are not templates**: they belong to the **Service** category, or **Meta Business Agent** when generated by Meta's AI agent.
</Note>

## How Meta categorizes and recategorizes

<Steps>
  <Step title="At creation">
    You choose the category and Meta validates it. If you selected **Utility** and the content is Marketing, the template is approved directly as **Marketing**. For other mismatches, the template is **rejected** with the reason `INCORRECT_CATEGORY`.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Periodic review">
    Approved templates are reviewed periodically. A Utility template that should be Marketing is recategorized with **1 day of notice** and stays approved.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Templates that should be Authentication">
    A Marketing or Utility template that should be Authentication **is not recategorized**: it is **rejected on the 1st day of the following month** and can no longer be sent.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review request">
    You can request a category review in **WhatsApp Manager** within **60 days** of the template's creation or, if it was recategorized, of the date of the change.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Info>
  Recategorization notices are sent by **email**, through a **banner in WhatsApp Manager**, and through Meta's `template_category_update` webhook, which {projectName} forwards as [`UPDATE_TEMPLATE_CATEGORY`](/en/webhooks/template-payloads).
</Info>

## Restrictions for Utility misuse

If the account frequently categorizes Marketing as Utility, Meta applies **escalating restrictions**:

| Level | What happens | Duration |
|---|---|---|
| **Warning** | Utility → Marketing changes become immediate, with no prior notice | Ongoing |
| **Sending limit** | Utility volume is capped in a rolling 24h window; excess messages are rejected | At least 7 days |
| **Utility restriction** | All Utility templates become Marketing; creating new Utility templates and requesting reviews are blocked | 7 days (30 for repeat violations) |
| **Portfolio restriction** | The same rule applies to all accounts in the portfolio | 30 days |

<Warning>
  Choose the correct category at creation. Categorizing Marketing as Utility does not lower the cost: the template is recategorized and the account may be restricted.
</Warning>
