## What is the 24-hour window?

It is the **customer service window** defined by Meta. It opens when the **customer sends a message or calls** your business, and each new message or call **resets the timer** to 24 hours.

While it is open, your business can reply with **free-form messages**.

### Example

| Time | Event | Window |
|---|---|---|
| 10:00 | Customer sends "Hi" | Open until 10:00 the next day |
| 15:30 | Business replies | ✅ Allowed (service message) |
| 18:20 | Customer sends another message | Now expires at 18:20 the next day |
| 09:00 the next day | Business replies | ✅ Allowed (service message) |
| 18:21 the next day | Business tries a free-form message | ❌ Rejected: template only |

## When the window is open

You can send any of these formats **without a template**:

- Text
- Image
- Video
- Audio
- Document
- Sticker
- Location
- Contact
- Buttons
- Lists
- Chatbot replies
- Messages from third-party AI agents
- Messages from human agents

<Note>
  These messages belong to the **Service** category: the first **1,000 per month, per business phone number**, are free. After that, each delivered message is charged. Eligible government and non-profit organizations follow a separate rule. See [Billing](/en/whatsapp-official/billing).
</Note>

<Warning>
  Replies generated by the **Meta Business Agent** are not in the Service category: they have their own category and pricing. See [Billing](/en/whatsapp-official/billing).
</Warning>

## When the window expires

After **24 hours with no message or call from the customer**, the window closes. From then on:

- Free-form messages are **rejected by the API**
- To reach the customer, you must send an **approved template**, charged by category (except in the free entry point window)

## What about the "72-hour window"?

The free entry point window, which opens when the customer comes from an ad, used to last **72 hours**. Meta extended it to **up to 7 days**, and that is what the official documentation describes today.

<Note>
  Meta has not published a rollout schedule for this change. If your account still behaves like 72 hours, confirm with Meta before planning sends around the 7 days.
</Note>

Meta's current rule is:

<Steps>
  <Step title="The customer comes from an ad">
    The customer clicks a **Click-to-WhatsApp (CTWA)** ad using the WhatsApp app on **Android or iOS**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="The business replies within 24h">
    The reply must happen within the 24h customer service window.
  </Step>
  <Step title="The free entry point window opens">
    Starting from that reply, the free entry point window opens and can last **up to 7 days**, with free **Marketing**, **Utility**, **Authentication**, and **Service** messages (except Meta Business Agent).
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Warning>
  **7 days is the maximum, not a guarantee.** The window may close earlier, so send important follow-ups as soon as possible.
</Warning>

<Warning>
  If the business **does not reply within 24h**, the free entry point window does not open.
</Warning>

<Note>
  The free entry point window **does not change the sending rule**: free-form messages still require the 24h window to be open. Once it closes, send a template, which is free while the free entry point window is open.
</Note>

## FAQ

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Can I send a regular message after 24 hours?">
    No. Send an approved template.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="The customer replied to the template. Can I chat normally?">
    Yes. The customer's reply opens a new 24-hour window.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Does every customer message reset the window?">
    Yes. Customer calls also reset the window.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Are messages inside the window free?">
    Only the **first 1,000 service messages per month, per business phone number**, or all of them if the free entry point window is open (except Meta Business Agent messages).
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Is a template sent inside the window free?">
    No. Marketing and Authentication were already charged. Utility templates inside the window were free from July 1, 2025 to September 30, 2026 and, since **October 1, 2026**, are also charged. The exception is the free entry point window.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Does the free entry point window always last 7 days?">
    No. 7 days is the maximum, not a guarantee: Meta documents that the window **may** remain open for up to 7 days.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Summary

| Situation | Can I send a regular message? | Template required? | Does Meta charge? |
|---|---|---|---|
| Customer messaged in the last 24h | ✅ Yes | ❌ No | Only after 1,000 service messages in the month |
| Window expired | ❌ No | ✅ Yes | ✅ Yes, by template category |
| Customer replied to the template | ✅ Yes | ❌ No | The template was charged; replies follow the service rule |
| Free entry point window open | ✅ Yes, while the 24h window is open | ✅ Yes, after the 24h window closes | ❌ No, except Meta Business Agent |

## Best practices

- Reply to **CTWA ad leads within 24h** to open the free entry point window
- Don't count on the full 7 days: send important follow-ups **early** in the free entry point window
- Monitor service message volume per number to know when the free tier runs out
- Keep an **active payment method**: without one, service messages stop being delivered after the 1,000 free ones
- Use templates only to start or resume a conversation, in the correct category (**Marketing**, **Utility**, or **Authentication**)
